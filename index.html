01 · Services

Four layers of care.

  1. 01

    Paint protection film

    A clear, tough film laid over the paint. It takes the stone chips, light scratches and road grime so the factory paint underneath doesn't have to.

    Ask for a quote →
  2. 02

    Ceramic coating

    A liquid coating that bonds to the paint and cures into a hard, glossy layer. Water beads and rolls off, and washing gets easier.

    Ask for a quote →
  3. 03

    Window tint

    Film applied to the glass to cut glare and heat from the sun and to add privacy inside the car.

    Ask for a quote →
  4. 04

    High-level detailing

    Careful cleaning and paint care, inside and out, to bring a car back to its best, and to prepare it before film or coating goes on.

    Ask for a quote →
